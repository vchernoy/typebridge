Settings

Transliteration shortcut

Choose a shortcut, or disable it. Shortcuts may be reserved by your operating system.

Transliteration rules

One rule per line: Latin = Cyrillic. Edit defaults or add rules. Changes affect future typing, not existing text.

Saving

On: saves text in this browser as you type. Off: privacy mode — text and title are not saved and will be lost on reload (including when a phone browser discards a background tab). This choice applies to every window; turning it off here does not offer to delete a saved copy (use Settings on the standalone editor for that).

Editor

When on, Tab and Shift+Tab indent lines in the editor. Shift+Tab on an unindented line, or turning this off, moves focus as usual.

Appearance

System follows your device. Your choice is saved in this browser.

Auto picks free windows on a computer pointer and stacked full-width windows on a phone-sized viewport.

Defaults

Documents and your Autosave privacy choice are kept.

About your documents

Saved documents stay in this browser only and are not synced between devices. Undo history is saved with documents. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; export important work.

Resize the editor from its bottom-right corner. After a successful online service-worker installation, the editor can reopen offline on this device.
