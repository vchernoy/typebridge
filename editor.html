Saving

When on, every document is private — text and title are not saved and will be lost on reload (including when a phone browser discards a background tab). The Autosave checkbox beside the title is per document and is locked off while a private session is on.

Editor

When on, Tab and Shift+Tab indent lines in the editor. Shift+Tab on an unindented line, or turning this off, moves focus as usual.
